Honest limits

A smaller file, not an anonymity guarantee

The cleaner recognizes common identifier and location column names, but vendors can invent new ones. Free text and unusual measurements may be identifying even when they are not labeled as such.

Apple Health support covers <Record> entries. Workouts, routes, clinical records, ActivitySummary, and nested metadata are intentionally not included in v1.
