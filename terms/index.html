Terms

This free utility helps you minimize a copy of your own health export. It is not medical advice, a security certification, or an anonymization service.

Your responsibility

  • Use files you own or have permission to process.
  • Review the cleaned CSV, file details, and risk note before sharing.
  • Keep a separate original export; the cleaner does not provide backup or recovery.
  • Comply with any rules that apply to the data and intended recipient.

Limitations

Vendor formats change. The cleaner detects common identifier and location field names but cannot recognize every sensitive value or guarantee that a recipient cannot re-identify someone. Apple Health XML support is limited to Record elements; the interface states other omissions before export.
